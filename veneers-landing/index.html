Our approach

Conservative dentistry. Considered cosmetics.

Veneers should enhance your natural smile, not replace it. Wherever clinically appropriate, Dr Aodhan uses a preservation-first technique that protects healthy enamel and gives your teeth a meaningful future, not just a result for today.

Important to know

Before you proceed.

Veneers are an elective procedure with permanent consequences in many cases. The points below are not the small print. They are the conversation we have with you in person before any treatment is planned.

01
Often irreversibleTraditional veneers require enamel removal which cannot be undone. Some cases may be reversible, assessed clinically.
02
SensitivityShort‑term sensitivity is common after preparation. Long‑term sensitivity is uncommon but possible.
03
Replacement over timeVeneers typically last 10 to 15 years. Eventual replacement should be planned and budgeted for.
04
Breakage & debondingVeneers can chip, fracture or debond under heavy bite forces. A custom nightguard is generally recommended.
05
Aesthetic variationFinal appearance depends on starting tooth structure, gum line, lip position and lighting.
06
SuitabilityNot every patient is a candidate. Active gum disease, decay or unmanaged bite issues must be addressed first.

Any dental procedure carries risk. Before proceeding we recommend seeking a second opinion from a suitably qualified dental practitioner. Outcomes vary between individuals; the cases shown on this website are examples for those specific patients and are not a guarantee of result for any other patient.
