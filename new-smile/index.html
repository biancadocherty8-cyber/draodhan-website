What to expect

From first visit to final smile

01
Consultation & planning
Dr Aodhan listens to what you would like to change, examines your teeth and gums, takes digital scans and talks through whether veneers or another option suits you.
02
Design & preview
Your smile is designed around your face and shown to you before anything is permanent. Shape, length and proportion are refined together until you are happy.
03
Placement & aftercare
Veneers are fitted conservatively, with as much natural tooth kept as possible. You leave with clear aftercare and ongoing reviews to keep your smile healthy.

Timeframes and suitability are individual and confirmed at your consultation. Veneers are an irreversible procedure and are not right for everyone.
