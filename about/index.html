About · Circular Quay

Dr Aodhan Docherty

Dr Aodhan Docherty is a Sydney dentist with a clinical focus on cosmetic and restorative dentistry. At Circular Quay, he personally assesses, plans and delivers treatment, from porcelain veneers and composite bonding to dental implants.

Important. The information on this page is general only and is not a substitute for an in-person clinical assessment. All dental procedures carry risks, and outcomes vary between individuals and cannot be guaranteed.

One dentist, start to finish

The dentist who assesses your teeth is the dentist who plans and provides your treatment. This continuity allows decisions about tooth preparation, materials and the final restoration to be considered together.

The Aodhan Method

His approach to veneers follows five stages: Understand, Design, Preview, Preserve and Refine. The method includes a trial smile in suitable cases and tooth preparation planned individually. It is a clinical approach, not a guarantee of outcome.

Dentist and educator

Aodhan graduated from the University of Sydney with honours and a University Medal. He is a university examiner in oral implants and co-author of Practical Procedures in Implant Dentistry. He founded Exceptional Dental Academy, where he teaches other dentists.

Implant training

Aodhan holds a Graduate Diploma in Clinical Dentistry (Oral Implants) from the University of Sydney.

Starting treatment

Patients can begin with a preliminary Smile Discussion or arrange a comprehensive appointment in person or by Zoom. An in-person clinical assessment is required before definitive treatment decisions.

Professional registration

Dr Aodhan Docherty is registered as a dentist with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (Ahpra). Registration number: DEN0001938737.
