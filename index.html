About

The dentist who
teaches other dentists

Dr Aodhan Docherty teaches aesthetic dentistry to practitioners across Australia and internationally. When you sit in his chair, you're with someone other dentists come to learn from.

His practice at Circular Quay is built around knowing each patient's case deeply. The dentist who assesses you is the one who treats you — which means nothing gets lost between appointments.

His approach to veneers is minimal prep — preserving as much of your natural tooth structure as possible while achieving the aesthetic outcome you're looking for.

Services

Every treatment, personally by Dr Docherty

Suitability is determined at an in-person consultation. Individual results vary and cannot be guaranteed.

01
Porcelain Veneers

A long-lasting, natural-looking option for changing the colour, shape, or alignment of teeth. Dr Docherty uses an ultra-thin, minimal prep approach — preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible. Porcelain veneers are the primary cosmetic treatment at this practice and where the majority of Dr Docherty's casework sits.

Learn more
02
Composite Bonding

A tooth-coloured resin applied and shaped directly on the tooth — no enamel removal required. A more affordable and reversible option for minor chips, gaps, or colour changes. Often a good starting point before committing to porcelain, or a standalone treatment for the right case.

Book a consultation
03
Invisalign

Clear aligner treatment to gradually move teeth. For many patients, Invisalign is worth considering before veneers — straightening first often means less intervention overall and a more natural result.

Book a consultation
04
Dental Crowns

Custom porcelain crowns for teeth that are damaged, heavily restored, or following implant or root canal treatment. Planned and placed by Dr Docherty at the Circular Quay clinic.

Book a consultation
05
Dental Implants

Implant and bridge treatment to replace missing teeth using digital planning and 3D technology. Suitability, risks, and alternatives are discussed at an in-person consultation.

How it works

A structured approach

Every treatment begins with understanding your individual situation. No procedure is recommended before a thorough assessment.

01
Book a Consultation

Book a 60-minute appointment at the Circular Quay clinic. You'll meet Dr Docherty, discuss what you're looking to achieve, and leave with a clear treatment plan and costs.

02
Clinical Assessment

Clinical photographs, digital scans, and a thorough assessment of your teeth, gums, and bite. No treatment is recommended before this is complete.

03
Treatment Planning

Options, realistic outcomes, costs, and alternatives are discussed in full. There is no obligation to proceed.

04
Informed Consent

Risks and alternatives are documented and consented to before any treatment begins.
