What we do

Cosmetic dentistry, done conservatively

Porcelain veneers are Dr Aodhan's focus, alongside a full range of cosmetic treatments. Every plan starts with what will keep your teeth healthiest for the long term.

Composite bonding
Tooth-coloured composite to reshape, close small gaps or repair edges, often in a single visit. A conservative way to refine a smile.

Professional whitening tailored to your teeth, for a natural, even brightness. Often the first step before any other cosmetic work.

What to expect

From first visit to final smile

A clear, unhurried path. You decide whether to proceed at every stage.

01
Consultation & planning
Dr Aodhan listens to what you'd like to change, examines your teeth and gums, takes digital scans and talks through whether veneers or another option suits you.
02
Design & preview
Your smile is designed around your face and shown to you before anything is permanent. Shape, length and proportion are refined together until you're happy.
03
Placement & aftercare
Veneers are fitted conservatively, with as much natural tooth kept as possible. You leave with clear aftercare and ongoing reviews to keep your smile healthy.

Timeframes and suitability are individual and confirmed at your consultation. Veneers are an irreversible procedure and are not right for everyone.
