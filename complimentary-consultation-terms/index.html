Consultation terms

Complimentary Smile Strategy Consultation

Here is exactly what your complimentary consultation includes, and what it does not, so you know what to expect before you book. In plain English.

It is free, with no obligation

The Complimentary Smile Strategy Consultation is provided at no cost. There is no obligation to book treatment, and no payment is required to attend.

What it is

A relaxed, in-person conversation with our Patient Care Coordinator at our Circular Quay practice, where you will also meet Dr Aodhan. We talk through what you would like to change, discuss your options, and take a few photos for reference. You leave with an indicative sense of direction and your next steps.

What it is not

It is an options and suitability discussion, not a clinical examination, diagnosis, or treatment. No dental treatment is provided on the day, and nothing said at this appointment should be taken as a formal clinical opinion.

If you decide to go further

A full clinical assessment, scans, and a written, personalised treatment plan are part of a separate comprehensive consultation. A fee applies for that appointment and is confirmed at the time of booking.

Suitability and results

Suitability for any treatment, including veneers, is confirmed only after an in-person clinical examination. Veneers and many cosmetic procedures are irreversible and are not right for everyone. All dental procedures carry potential risks, individual results vary, and outcomes cannot be guaranteed. Any before-and-after images shown are individual results and are not a promise of your own outcome.

Bookings, availability and cancellations

Complimentary consultations are subject to availability and are intended for people genuinely considering treatment. One per person. Because Dr Aodhan sees a limited number of patients, please give us reasonable notice if you need to change or cancel your appointment, so we can offer the time to someone else.
