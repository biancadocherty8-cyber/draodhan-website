About

The dentist who
teaches other dentists

Dr Aodhan Docherty graduated from the University of Sydney with honours and a University Medal, and is the founder of Exceptional Dental Academy, where he trains other dentists across Australia and internationally. He is a university examiner in oral implants and co-author of the dental textbook Practical Procedures in Implant Dentistry. When you sit in his chair, you're with someone other dentists come to learn from.

His practice at Circular Quay is built around knowing each patient's case deeply. The dentist who assesses you is the one who treats you, which means nothing gets lost between appointments.

His approach to veneers is conservative, focused on a natural result that still looks like you. Veneers are irreversible and not right for everyone, so suitability is always assessed in person, and he'll tell you if a simpler option suits you better.

Services

Every treatment, personally by Dr Docherty

Suitability is determined at an in-person consultation. Individual results vary and cannot be guaranteed.

Porcelain Veneers

A long-lasting, natural-looking option for changing the colour, shape, or alignment of teeth. Dr Docherty takes a conservative approach focused on a result that still looks like you. Porcelain veneers are the primary cosmetic treatment at this practice and where the majority of Dr Docherty's casework sits.

Learn more
Composite Bonding

A tooth-coloured resin applied and shaped directly on the tooth, with no enamel removal required. A more conservative and reversible option for minor chips, gaps, or colour changes. Often a good starting point before committing to porcelain, or a standalone treatment for the right case.

Learn more
Clear Aligners

Clear aligner treatment to gradually move teeth. For many patients, clear aligners are worth considering before veneers. Straightening first often means less intervention overall and a more natural result.

Learn more
General Dentistry

Check-ups, hygiene, fillings and crowns, the everyday care that keeps your smile healthy. Prevention first, treating only what needs it, all at the Circular Quay clinic.

Dental Implants

Implant and bridge treatment to replace missing teeth using digital planning and 3D technology. Suitability, risks, and alternatives are discussed at an in-person consultation.

How it works

A structured approach
