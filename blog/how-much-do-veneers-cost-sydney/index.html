How much do veneers cost in Sydney? What changes the price?

Home » Journal » How much do veneers cost in Sydney? What changes the price?
By Dr Aodhan Docherty

Veneer costs vary depending on the number of teeth, material and clinical complexity. A written treatment plan is needed to identify the procedures and associated fees for your individual case. See veneer costs.

Why costs differ

Porcelain and composite restorations involve different materials, techniques and laboratory requirements. Existing fillings, tooth alignment and the number of teeth being treated can affect the work required.

What the Aodhan Method includes

Dr Aodhan’s veneer process involves consultation and scans, treatment planning, a temporary trial smile in suitable cases, final fitting and ongoing review. The trial smile is commonly worn for approximately three weeks, so patients can experience the proposed shape before final restorations are fitted.

What to compare in a quote

Ask which diagnostics, temporary restorations, laboratory work, final restorations and reviews are included. Also ask about repair, maintenance and replacement costs over time.

Medicare and private health funds

Cosmetic dental treatment is not covered by Medicare. Any private health fund rebate depends on the fund, policy and item.

Frequently asked questions

Can I receive a veneer quote from photos?
Photographs can help communicate concerns, but an accurate quote generally requires clinical assessment.

Does the cost include a trial smile?
The written plan should specify whether a temporary trial smile is indicated and included.

Is porcelain always the right choice?
No. Composite bonding, aligners or no treatment may be more appropriate in some cases.

Before you proceed. Veneers may require irreversible tooth preparation and can involve sensitivity, fracture, debonding or future replacement. Outcomes vary and suitability requires examination. This article is general information only and not personal dental advice.
