A signature approach

The Aodhan Method™

A considered, preservation-first way of planning and placing porcelain veneers — guided by the same dentist from your very first conversation to your final smile. It is less a product and more a philosophy: design around your face, preserve healthy tooth, and never finalise anything until you feel confident.

1
Understand

It begins with your face, not just your teeth. Dr Aodhan listens to what you want your smile to say, and studies your features — lip line, proportions, the way you speak and smile — so the design is built around you rather than dropped onto a template.

2
Design

Using a blend of digital planning and hand design, your proposed smile is shaped around your facial proportions — balancing tooth shape, length, width and harmony as a whole, not tooth by tooth. This is where artistry and dentistry meet.

3
Preview

You see, and can often trial, your proposed smile before any irreversible treatment begins. It is a chance to live with the design, refine it, and only move forward once it genuinely feels like you. Nothing is finalised until you are confident in the plan.

4
Preserve

Teeth are prepared as conservatively as each individual case allows, protecting as much healthy, natural tooth structure as possible. This is the heart of the method — because what is removed cannot be put back, restraint is treated as a skill, not a shortcut.

5
Refine

Each veneer is hand-characterised by skilled ceramic technicians and refined at the fit appointment, adjusting shade, texture and edge until the finished result reads as a natural part of you — not as dental work.

Why it matters

Built to keep your smile feeling like yours

Many smiles can be transformed quickly. The harder, more valuable goal is a result that still looks and feels natural in five, ten, twenty years — and that depends on how much healthy tooth is preserved and how carefully the design suits your face.

The Aodhan Method exists to make that the default, not the exception. Every stage is designed to slow the irreversible decisions down, keep you in control of the plan, and protect the foundation your smile is built on.

The Aodhan Method describes Dr Aodhan's personal approach to planning veneer treatment. It is not a brand of product, a trademark of any outcome, or a guarantee of any result.
