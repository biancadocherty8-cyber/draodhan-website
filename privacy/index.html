Privacy policy

This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you use this website or contact the practice. We handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). In plain English.

Who we are

This website is operated for Dr Aodhan Docherty, a cosmetic dentist practising at About Smiles Circular Quay, Ground Floor, Shop LLC125, 17 Young St, Sydney NSW 2000. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to the practice of Dr Aodhan Docherty.

What we collect

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to respond to you and provide dental services. Depending on how you use the site, this may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number, when you complete the smile assessment or a contact or booking form.
  • The answers you give in the smile assessment, such as what you would like to change about your smile and your general timing.
  • Any information you choose to include in a message to us, and a photo of your smile if you choose to upload one.
  • Technical information collected automatically, such as your device type, browser, approximate location and how you interact with the pages.

You do not have to provide your personal information, but if you do not, we may not be able to respond to your enquiry or arrange an appointment.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange and manage your appointment, provide dental care and information, improve this website and our services, and, where you would expect it, to let you know about relevant services. We use it only for the purposes for which it was provided or for related purposes you would reasonably expect.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to measure and improve our advertising. These tools may collect information such as pages viewed, clicks, and general location, and may set cookies on your device. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

Third parties who help us run the site

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the website and our enquiry process. When you use these features, your information is handled by these providers under their own privacy terms:

  • JotForm, which processes the smile assessment and enquiry form submissions.
  • Zavy360, which powers online appointment booking.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), through the Meta pixel and lead forms, for advertising and measuring the results of our ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity, for anonymous, aggregated analytics such as heatmaps and session trends.
  • Google, for search, analytics and advertising services where used.

These providers may store or process information outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to use reputable providers, but we do not control their own handling of your information.

When we disclose information

We do not sell your personal information. We only disclose it to the service providers above so they can perform their function, to other health practitioners involved in your care with your consent, or where we are required or authorised to do so by law.

How we store and protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, and to keep it only as long as it is needed or as required by law. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information at every step.

Marketing and how to opt out

If we send you information about our services, you can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting the practice. We will action your request promptly.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Contact the practice using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. There is normally no charge to make a request.

Questions or complaints

If you have a question or a concern about how we have handled your information, please contact the practice first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
